# Bill of Materials (2025) <!-- omit in toc -->

Parts list for one 2025 board: Raspberry Pi Pico 2, DFPlayer Mini and a 300-pixel NeoPixel strip. Quantities are per board, except the fuses, which cover all three 2025 boards. The 2026 two-rig design changes these counts; see the [2026 blueprint](../explanation/2026-blueprint.md#41-hardware).

## Table of Contents <!-- omit in toc -->

- [1. Parts](#1-parts)

## 1. Parts

- 2 × 5V 8–10A power supplies (one per 150-pixel segment)
  - https://www.amazon.ca/dp/B0DYHCD21G
- 1 × 12V 5A power supply (amplifier)
  - https://www.amazon.ca/dp/B01GEA8PQA
- Barrel jack to screw terminal adapters (female) — 2 per board for NeoPixels
  - https://www.amazon.ca/dp/B0BHYTRP1M
- Barrel jack (PCB-mount male) — 1 per amp
- 2-position green screw terminal blocks (amplifier input & speaker output)
  - https://www.amazon.ca/dp/B088LSS14J (2 per amp)
- 18 AWG silicone stranded wire (red + black) — 10 m total
  - https://www.amazon.ca/dp/B0C7T5H9DP
- 18 AWG ferrules — 30 pcs
- 10A fuses (inline or fuse holder) — 6 pcs (one per 5V supply)
- 5A fuses — 3 pcs (one per 12V supply)
  - https://www.amazon.ca/dp/B07F8RLMPB
- Heat shrink kit
  - https://www.amazon.ca/dp/B08XXGNJHG
